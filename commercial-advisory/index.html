Keith Bainbridge

Keith Bainbridge is an independent LNG shipping and floating infrastructure specialist with more than 40 years of maritime and energy industry experience.

His career spans LNG shipping operations, vessel acquisition, chartering, floating LNG infrastructure, project development, transportation strategy and commercial advisory.

As founder of CS LNG Limited, Keith advises energy companies, investors, project sponsors, utilities and shipowners on LNG transportation arrangements, FSRU developments, shipping strategy and commercial execution.

Prior to establishing CS LNG, Keith held senior leadership positions including Partner and Head of Gas at RS Platou LLP, Managing Director of Clarkson LNG and LNG Shipping Solutions, and Shipping Manager for Nigeria LNG.

His experience combines commercial, operational and project execution perspectives developed over several decades in the LNG sector.

Keith continues to support clients on LNG shipping, floating infrastructure, vessel acquisition, transportation strategy and commercially significant project decisions through CS LNG's independent advisory practice.
