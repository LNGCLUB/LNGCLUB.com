WHY IT MATTERS

Why LNG & Floating Infrastructure Matter

LNG and floating infrastructure provide practical, scalable and rapid solutions for energy security, supply diversification and resilience.

Why LNG Matters

LNG enables countries and companies to diversify energy supplies, improve energy security and access global gas markets when domestic supply is insufficient.

It provides flexibility, resilience and optionality in a rapidly changing geopolitical and commercial environment.

Why Floating Infrastructure Matters

  • Rapid deployment compared with onshore import terminals
  • Lower upfront capital requirements
  • Increased flexibility and mobility
  • Ability to respond quickly to changing supply needs
  • Reduced development and permitting timelines

Key Benefits

  • Energy security and resilience
  • Supply diversification
  • Infrastructure flexibility
  • Faster implementation
  • Commercial optionality
