INDEPENDENCE & CONFLICTS

Independence & Conflict Management

Independent, non-broker advisory and expert support delivered with professional independence, discretion and conflict management.

Independent Positioning

CS LNG acts exclusively on an independent and non-broker basis.

The practice does not act as a broker, owner, trader or transaction counterparty and does not participate in asset ownership, chartering or fee-based deal execution.

Engagement Basis

  • Without brokerage, ownership or transactional conflicts
  • Client-specific engagements with conflict reviews conducted as appropriate
  • Professional independence and discretion throughout the engagement
  • Confidential handling of all enquiries and appointments

Conflict Review & Declarations

Where required for expert appointments, arbitration support or dispute-related engagements, formal declarations of independence and conflict status can be provided.

Prospective engagements remain subject to conflict review and suitability assessment.
